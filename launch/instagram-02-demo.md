# Instagram post 2 — product demonstration

## Objective

Prove that the product works by showing an authentic result.

## Format

A short Reel is preferred. A screen-recording carousel can be used as a
fallback.

## Opening frame

Can ChatGPT create three dinners using this week's grocery deals?

## Demonstration sequence

1. Open ChatGPT with kortingzoeker.com enabled.
2. Enter:

   > Create three healthy dinners for four people for under €40 using current
   > Picnic deals.

3. Show kortingzoeker.com retrieving deals.
4. Show the resulting meal ideas.
5. Show the combined grocery list.
6. Highlight products whose prices came from the deal results.
7. End with:

   > What would you ask it?

Use a real result recorded on the publication date. Do not mock, edit, or
manually improve the output.
