## Caption

Finding deals is not the hardest part. Deciding what to make with them is.

With kortingzoeker.com, you can ask ChatGPT to create meal ideas and grocery
lists using available Picnic deals.

For example:

> Create three healthy dinners for four people for under €40.

We have just launched, and we would love to hear what could be improved.

🔗 Try it through the link in our bio  
🛒 Picnic is the first supported supermarket
